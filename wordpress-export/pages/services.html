What We Do

A Complete Service, Not Just a Product

Every project moves through the same disciplined process, whether it's a single aisle of shelving or a full-scale distribution centre fit-out.

Site Survey & Consultation

We visit your facility to assess space, structural constraints and workflow before recommending a system that actually fits how you operate.

Custom Racking Design

Engineered layouts tailored to your load profile, pallet sizes and throughput targets, delivered with full CAD drawings and load calculations.

Supply & Manufacturing

Premium-grade steel racking manufactured to your exact specification under strict quality control, and delivered on schedule.

Professional Installation

Certified installation crews fit your system to code, with minimal disruption to your daily warehouse operations.

Safety Inspection & Certification

Routine load-capacity inspections and compliance checks that keep your racking safe, certified and insurance-ready.

Maintenance & After-Sales Support

Ongoing maintenance, replacement parts and responsive technical support long after your system is installed.
