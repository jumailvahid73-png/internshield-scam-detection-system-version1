5 Signs Your Warehouse Needs a Racking Upgrade

Home/Blog/5 Signs Your Warehouse Needs a Racking Upgrade
June 2, 2026 4 min read Orient Tech Team

Most warehouses don't outgrow their racking overnight -- it happens gradually, one extra pallet at a time, until the signs become impossible to ignore. If any of the following sound familiar, it's worth having your storage layout reviewed before small inefficiencies turn into real operational costs.

1. Pallets are stored on the floor

If staff are stacking overflow stock in the aisles or against the walls, your racking capacity has already been exceeded. Floor storage isn't just inefficient -- it's a safety hazard and it slows down every pick that has to route around it.

2. Picking takes longer than it used to

A racking layout that made sense for last year's SKU mix can quietly become a bottleneck as your product range grows. If pickers are walking further, double-handling stock, or hunting for space to place new deliveries, the layout -- not the team -- is usually the problem.

3. Damaged frames or beams are piling up

Dented uprights, bent beams and missing safety pins are more than cosmetic. Damaged racking loses load capacity, and once a system has been patched and re-patched over several years, replacement is almost always cheaper than continued repair.

4. You're paying for extra warehouse space

Renting or building additional space is often the most expensive way to solve a storage shortage. A denser racking system -- taller selective racking, drive-in bays, or a mezzanine floor -- can frequently recover 30-50% more usable storage within your existing footprint.

5. Your last safety inspection flagged issues

If an inspection has noted overloading, missing signage or structural wear, treat it as a planning opportunity, not just a compliance box to tick. A racking upgrade at that point can be designed around your current operation rather than the constraints of a system installed years ago.

If two or more of these sound familiar, a short site visit is usually enough to tell you whether you need a full redesign or a smaller, targeted fix. Get in touch and our team will assess your space at no cost.
