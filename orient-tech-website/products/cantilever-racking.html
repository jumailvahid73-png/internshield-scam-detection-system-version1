Cantilever Racking

Cantilever racking removes front-facing columns entirely, giving unobstructed access to long or awkward loads such as timber, pipe and steel bar. Adjustable arms let you tailor storage levels to your exact product range, while the heavy-duty column design supports substantial per-arm load ratings.
