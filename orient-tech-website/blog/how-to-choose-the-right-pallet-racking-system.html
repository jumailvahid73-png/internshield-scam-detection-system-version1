How to Choose the Right Pallet Racking System for Your Business

Home/Blog/How to Choose the Right Pallet Racking System for Your Business
June 18, 2026 5 min read Orient Tech Team

With more than a dozen racking types on the market, choosing the right one usually comes down to answering a handful of practical questions about how your warehouse actually runs -- not which system looks the most impressive in a catalogue.

Start with your SKU variety

If you handle a wide range of products with frequent picking, selective pallet racking is almost always the right starting point. It gives 100% direct access to every pallet position, which matters more than raw density when your stock mix changes often.

Then consider throughput vs. density

If you're storing large volumes of a small number of SKUs -- frozen goods, beverages, raw materials -- drive-in or drive-through racking can dramatically increase density by removing aisles between bays. The trade-off is reduced selectivity, so this only pays off when product variety is low.

Long or awkward loads need their own category

Timber, steel bar, pipe and furniture don't fit conventional beams. Cantilever racking removes the front columns entirely, giving unobstructed access to long items that would otherwise overhang a standard bay.

Running out of height, not floor space?

A mezzanine floor effectively doubles your usable area within the same building envelope, and can carry its own racking or workstations. It's often the fastest way to add capacity without a single extra square metre of land.

A simple way to decide

  • High SKU variety, frequent picking → Selective pallet racking
  • Few SKUs, high volume, low picking frequency → Drive-in or drive-through racking
  • Long, bulky or irregular loads → Cantilever racking
  • Limited floor space but spare height → Mezzanine floor
  • Fast-changing inventory in a compact footprint → Mobile shelving

Most warehouses end up using a combination of two or three of these systems rather than a single type throughout. If you're not sure where your operation fits, send us your warehouse details and we'll recommend a layout during a free site visit.
