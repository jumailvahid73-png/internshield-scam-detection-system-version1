About Container Solutions

One of the Region's Most Trusted Container Specialists

Established in 2003 in Dubai, UAE, we are a leading provider of container solutions. Our activities span container conversion, modular building manufacturing, offshore container manufacturing, container leasing, depot services, and container trading across the Middle East and beyond.

From a 20,000 sq.m. facility in Dubai to over USD 100 million in turnover today, we serve prestigious clients across the region and internationally — with specialist expertise managing Oil & Gas and Military projects.

Our Mission

Reliable partners providing consistent quality, timely delivery, and a professional work culture built on expertise.

Our Vision

To be the leading container solutions provider in the Middle East, South, and Central Asia.
